# TL;DR

The decibel scale hurts more than it helps.
If you need to write large numbers next to tiny numbers, then use scientific notation.
If you need to plot large numbers next to tiny numbers, then use a *logarithmic* scale.

# The Confusion

* Decibels are not units
* 20? 10?
* I thought that was for noise levels at sports games. Why are we talking about voltages?

# History

* Lazy engineers
* Power vs voltage. We equivocate electrical power with signal power and then we make a confusing system
* Multiplication was difficult in the past
* Plotting was difficult in the past
* Now it's a shibboleth
